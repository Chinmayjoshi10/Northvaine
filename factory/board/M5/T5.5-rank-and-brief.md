## Goal
Render what the founder actually judges: a ranked, readable brief per client where every fact
is a verified quote, every entry states its stream and window, and a scorecard to rate it.
Code only; no model call.

## Contract
- `rank(passed, *, as_of, settings)`: components
  1. strength: `strong` = verified claims from at least 2 documents, or at least 3 claims from a
     structured record; `medium` = at least 2 claims; else `weak`;
  2. urgency: open tender window (fewer days left first) > dated within `radar_recent_days` >
     undated > closed window;
  3. direct buyer: `gate.direct_buyer is True` first.
  Sort lexicographically by (strength, urgency, direct buyer), then name for stability. Each
  component becomes one plain `rank_reasons` line (e.g. "Evidence: 3 verified claims from 1
  official record", "Window: tender closes in 12 days (stated deadline)", "Buyer: buys directly").
  No numeric score is computed or shown.
- `render_brief(run)`: header (client id, offer, as_of, documents by kind, leads, upgrades
  succeeded/refused, LLM calls and cost); a "Streams" paragraph (tender and award are enumerable:
  every matching notice in the query window was read; announcements are not: some, never all);
  top `radar_brief_entries` entries, each with the nine numbered questions:
  1 Who (name, country name, roles) · 2 What happened (event descriptions + refs) · 3 When
  (dates; stream; window with "estimated"/"stated", or the window note) · 4 Why it creates demand
  (gate demand reason, labelled assessment, with refs, or "not stated") · 5 Evidence (each claim:
  statement, then the verbatim quote as a blockquote, with ref) · 6 Sources (URL, source name,
  available date, "official record"/"full page"/"page fetched from a search lead") · 7 Why a
  buyer, not a competitor or publisher (gate buyer reason + refs, direct or via others) · 8 What
  is uncertain (skeptic points with refs, open questions, plus code flags: estimated window,
  single document, claimed date untrusted for upgraded leads) · 9 Suggested action (gate
  suggested action, or "not stated"; followed by "A person decides and makes contact.").
  Then a "Thin sources" paragraph when fewer than `radar_brief_entries` passed (correction 4),
  an "Also passed" one-line list beyond the top N, and a "Rejected" table (name, rejected_as,
  by, reason; persons shown as "(a person, name withheld)").
- `render_scorecard(run)`: the PLAN.md go/no-go instructions in short, then one table row per
  briefed entry with empty Correct / Relevant / New-or-worth-acting-on / Actionable / Failure chip
  cells; the third column header depends on nothing in code: it prints both wordings and the rule
  "stone client: previously unknown; other clients: would a salesperson act on this?" as text.
- `write_reports(run, out_dir)`: `out_dir/<client_id>/<as_of date>/{brief.md, scorecard.md,
  run.json}`; returns the paths; overwrites the same day's files.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `tests/radar/test_brief.py` on a synthetic `RadarRun` (invented names): ranking order and
      reason lines for strength, open-vs-closed window and direct buyer; ties broken by name.
- [ ] Every entry block contains the nine numbered headings, the stream word and a window line.
- [ ] Every blockquote in the brief equals a `CandidateClaim.source_quote` of that entry, and
      every `[Cn]` ref in the entry exists on its candidate.
- [ ] A gate field that is `None` renders "not stated"; no model text appears outside sections
      4, 7, 8 and 9.
- [ ] Fewer passed entries than `radar_brief_entries` -> the thin-sources paragraph appears;
      rejected entries all appear in the table; a `not_an_organisation` row shows no name.
- [ ] `write_reports` writes three files to a `tmp_path`; `run.json` re-loads into `RadarRun`.
- [ ] Rendering is deterministic (same run -> byte-identical output).
- [ ] No import of `northvaine.brain.llm` (invariant rule); `tasks.py gate` green.
