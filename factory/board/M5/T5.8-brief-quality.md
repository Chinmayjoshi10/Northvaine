# T5.8 — Brief quality: stated or inferred, who buys, a short list, a one-screen summary (M5.1)

status: done
role: builder
wave: M5.1
depends: T5.0-T5.7 (merged)

## Why
The founder, ChatGPT (`docs/COLLAB.md`, 5 Oct) and the CTO rated the first live briefs 4-5/10:
- generic works are promoted as a need for the offer;
- at tender stage the named authority is not the material buyer;
- the briefs are long and mechanical, with generic actions;
- tenders closing tomorrow rank first.

Everything below is general, not tuned to one industry. CLAUDE.md #1 applies: no industry words
in code, prompts or settings defaults.

## Changes
1. **Requirement: stated or inferred.**
   - `GateOutput` gains `requirement_ref: str | None`, placed after `needs_the_offer` and before
     `verdict`. It is the ref of the claim whose quote names what the client sells, or null.
   - Gate prompt v4 asks for it.
   - Code makes it `stated` only when `ref_of(requirement_ref)` is a real ref on the candidate
     AND that claim's quote contains at least one offer term. Offer terms are the content words
     of `spec.offer`, case-folded. Stopwords live in a settings list and are generic language
     words only.
   - Otherwise it is `inferred`.
   - Store it as `GateDecision.requirement: Literal["stated","inferred"] | None` and carry it to
     the entry.
2. **Who buys, by stream: data, not code.**
   - Add the setting `radar_buyer_note_by_stream: dict[str, str]`, with generic defaults:
     - **tender:** "The organisation named runs this tender. The goods or materials are often
       bought by the contractor that wins it. Reach likely bidders now, and check the
       specification."
     - **award:** "The winner named here carries out the contract and buys what it needs for it."
     - **announcement:** "The organisation named is acting on its own plans and is the likely
       buyer."
   - Brief section 7 shows the stream note before the model's sentence.
3. **Ranking.** Sort key, in order:
   - `stated` before `inferred`;
   - then open tender windows with at least `radar_min_days_left` days left (default 5);
   - then other dated entries;
   - then windows closing in under `radar_min_days_left` days, and closed windows, last;
   - then the existing evidence strength, recency and direct-buyer keys.

   Each "why it is ranked here" line names the requirement status and, when the window is too
   short, says "closes in N days: likely too late to prepare a bid".
4. **A short main list.** The brief's full entries are capped at `radar_brief_main_entries`
   (default 5), and only `stated` entries, or `strong` evidence entries, qualify. All other
   passed entries go in a table "Also found, weaker" (rank, company, stream, requirement, window,
   one-line reason). The thin-sources note applies when fewer than 3 full entries qualify.
5. **A one-screen summary at the top**, before everything else. It is a table of the full
   entries:

   | # | Company | What (title or first statement) | Requirement | Window / when | Why now |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|

   - "What" uses the claim whose quote starts with `Title [` when present, else the first
     verified statement, cut to about 90 characters.
   - "Why now" is code-composed from the window (for example "tender closes in 12 days" or
     "announced 2026-09-14").
   - No model text appears in this table.
6. **Plain "What happened".** For structured records, render one plain sentence from the title,
   buyer, publication date and deadline claims, each followed by its `[Cn]`, then the evidence
   list as before. Do not print the "The notice record states ..." concatenation in section 2.
7. **Suggested action.**
   - Show a stage line from the setting `radar_action_by_stream: dict[str, str]`, with generic
     defaults:
     - **tender:** "Before the deadline: find the likely bidders and offer them a quote they can
       price into their bid."
     - **award:** "Introduce yourself to the winner while it plans its purchases."
     - **announcement:** "Contact the organisation, or the people designing its project,
       before suppliers are chosen."
   - Show the model's suggestion only if it cites refs (D63 as now).
   - Never show a date, price or contact person in the stage line.
