## Goal
Prove, on the real recorded runs of all three clients, the properties the founder relies on
before the founder reads a brief: every fact is verified and present in its source, rejections are kept,
order holds, and the same code serves every client offline.

## Contract
No production code. Tests replay `tests/fixtures/radar/<client>/` exactly as `scripts/radar.py`
does (no `.env`, no network) and inspect the `RadarRun` and the rendered brief.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] For each client: the replay uses every recorded LLM exchange and page (nothing unused,
      nothing missing; a one-character document change raises a replay mismatch).
- [ ] Zero unsupported facts: every `CandidateClaim` is verified, its quote is found in its
      document's text by `find_quote` in its mode, and every blockquote in `brief.md` equals one
      of those quotes; every `[Cn]` resolves.
- [ ] Every number in brief sections 4, 7, 8, 9 appears in a quote cited in that entry.
- [ ] Every candidate is either an entry or a `RejectedEntry` (counts add up); no rejected
      candidate has a skeptic call; every entry has a `SkepticFinding`.
- [ ] Every entry states a stream and a window line; tender entries have window dates; enumerable
      wording appears only on tender/award entries.
- [ ] No email or phone pattern in any recording, `run.json` or brief; no `not_an_organisation`
      name in a brief.
- [ ] Generality: the same function call for all three clients; packaging with fewer than
      `radar_brief_entries` entries shows the thin-sources paragraph.
- [ ] `tests/golden/radar_expectations.json` (orchestrator-reviewed counts per client: documents,
      leads, upgraded, candidates, rejected by reason, entries) matches the replay; the golden
      runs in `tasks.py golden`.
- [ ] Prints a per-client table (documents, leads, upgraded, candidates, rejected by reason,
      briefed, LLM calls, cost) for the M5 report.
- [ ] `tasks.py gate` green.

## Invariants touched
Rules #2, #3, #4, #7, #9 and personal-data rules are asserted on real data; point-in-time:
upgraded documents' `available_at` equals their `fetched_at`.
