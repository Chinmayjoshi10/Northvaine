### T1.7 blocker

The integrator ran db-test after the merge and it failed. The role says to drop a red merge
with `git reset --hard ee6c3c4`, but the permission classifier denied that command. So main
still contains the T1.7 merge, and the T1.7 worktree was kept.

I reproduced the failure on main:

```
FAILED tests/db/test_queue_db.py::test_run_worker_end_to_end
    assert all((await row(maker, i))["status"] == "done" for i in ids)
E   TypeError: 'async_generator' object is not iterable          (tests/db/test_queue_db.py:161)
1 failed, 11 passed, 211 deselected in 575.72s
```

**Root cause: the test is wrong, not the queue.** An `await` inside a generator expression
turns it into an async generator, which `all()` cannot iterate. The two assertions before it
pass: `run_worker` returned 3 and handled every enqueued id. The other queue db tests also pass
on the live database:
- the InitPlan claim (checked with EXPLAIN);
- two claimers never sharing a job;
- retry with backoff, then terminal failure;
- a non-owner cannot complete a job;
- stale leases are requeued or failed;
- a heartbeat prevents a requeue.

**Fix (one line, in T1.7's `writes`):** collect the statuses first, then assert, for example
`statuses = [(await row(maker, i))["status"] for i in ids]` followed by
`assert all(s == "done" for s in statuses)`. Make this fix in the kept T1.7 worktree, then
re-review, re-gate and rerun db-test.

Decision for the founder or orchestrator:
- (a) keep dcd2cb4 and land the one-line test fix on top (recommended, because the queue code
  is proven by the other tests), or
- (b) drop the merge (`git reset --hard ee6c3c4`, a destructive operation that needs human
  approval) and re-merge the fixed branch.

The ruff gate missed this. Consider adding a ruff rule or a mypy check for it; see the
follow-ups.
