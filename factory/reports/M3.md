## Review notes (non-blocking)

1. `test_same_loop_serves_every_client` skips any query whose body was not recorded. That is
   right for the three refused Tavily recordings, but it would also silently skip a TED query
   whose recording went missing. Suggest asserting that every TED query is recorded (TED is
   enumerable and all 8 are present), or listing the expected unrecorded Tavily queries.
2. 9 of the 13 Tavily documents are snippet-only (`text_kind='snippet'`), so the M4 verifier
   will refuse most of them as quote sources (plan risk 10). Expected, but it limits what the
   announcement stream proves in the demo.
3. The registry builds `WebsiteSensor` without a `resolver`; fine for production (system DNS),
   tests inject theirs directly.
