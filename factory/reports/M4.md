## What exists

  normalized exact, then fuzzy >= 0.95 with numbers unchanged and the document's own span stored)
  before any model call; batched fast-tier support check over passing quotes only (D47, D48, D55).

- Unverified claims stored with a reason, read only through `verified_claims` (D49).

  level-0 concept from seeded data, deterministic + embedding clustering, idempotent fingerprints.
- Recorded Nemotron fixtures replay offline, independent of `.env` and model ids (D53).
